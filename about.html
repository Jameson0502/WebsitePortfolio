About Art Acquaint

I am an internet artist and emerging web designer based in Asheville, North Carolina. I earned my Associate in Arts degree from Asheville-Buncombe Technical Community College and am currently pursuing my Bachelor's degree in New Media at the University of North Carolina Asheville. While I work across a variety of digital mediums, I am first and foremost a web designer skilled in using HTML to build responsive, immersive web experiences.

Notable projects include Bits and Pieces, a site created for a physical art installation, and a nonlinear interactive site exploring the narrative experience of browsing the web. I also created and fully produced the short film Echoes of Steel, handling all animation, sound design, and editing myself.

Alongside my studies, I have maintained steady experience in retail and food service, building a strong work ethic, adaptability, and reliability along the way. I am seeking Web Developer positions that will allow me to craft story driven websites and contribute to every phase of the development process.
