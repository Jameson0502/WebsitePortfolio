Snoopy's House

Snoopy's House, 2026, 3D Model

Snoopy's House is a 3D model of the doghouse belonging to the iconic character Snoopy. The model is UV-mapped and lit. It sits in a moonlit night scene with a large full moon behind it, a brick wall and wooden fence closing in the yard, and a food bowl out front. The red roof is strung with glowing multicolored lights, and a bare, drooping tree with a single ornament stands to the right. The cool blue night palette contrasts with the warm glow of the lights.

The UV-mapping carries the wood-plank lines across the house and the textures for the brick, fence, and moon. The lighting does most of the mood work: the string lights glow against the dark roof, and the moonlight throws long shadows across the ground.
