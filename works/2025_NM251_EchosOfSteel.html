Echos Of Steel

Echos Of Steel, 2025, Multimedia Animation

Echoes of Steel is a 1-minute and 5-second multimedia animated short film that follows a protagonist stranded in a barren desert alongside someone who was once his ally. The narrative explores themes of isolation, tension, and fractured relationships within a stark, minimalist landscape.

Visually distinctive for its hybrid aesthetic, the film blends hand-drawn 2D framing with stylized environments and physical elements, capturing a desolate atmosphere through stark horizons and dramatic shifts in perspective. The production of Echoes of Steel utilizes a diverse, multidisciplinary workflow, combining Autodesk Maya for 3D model creation and environmental rendering with Adobe Animate for 2D character animation and framing sequences. Tactile stop-motion techniques are integrated directly into the pipeline to bring physical mechanical models to life within the narrative, highlighting a hands-on approach to mixed-media storytelling and asset integration.
