Joke Delivery

Joke Delivery, 2026, New Media Performance Art

Joke Delivery is an interactive performance that explores comedic timing, distraction, and the relationship between a performer and the technology working against them. I deliver a series of jokes to a seated audience while a projection behind me displays a live image of myself. As the performance progresses, Max/MSP begins introducing increasingly distracting visual distortions and audio effects. The humor does not come primarily from the jokes themselves, but from watching me gradually lose control of the performance as the system interferes with my delivery. Unable to see the projection behind me, I must rely on what I hear while continuing to face the audience.

The performance uses a microphone to capture my voice in real time, feeding it into a Max/MSP patch that controls audio processing and projected visual effects. Operating independently of my delivery, the patch follows a timed sequence divided into three stages, progressively intensifying the distortions as the performance continues. A joke counter and timer drive the system, creating an escalating series of interruptions that eventually interfere with my ability to maintain my place and deliver the material.
