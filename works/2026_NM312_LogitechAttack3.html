Attack Three

Attack Three, 2026, 3D Model

The Attack Three is a 3D model of a to-scale Logitech Attack 3 joystick controller. I recreated the object following exact measurements and with the utmost control. The geometric shape of the controller proved challenging but rewarding. The model captures the curved, glossy handle with its trigger cutout, along with the angular base, its buttons, and the Logitech logo and "ATTACK" label. It is shown on a white pedestal against a dark backdrop, with views from the side, a three-quarter angle, and directly overhead.

The model is fully UV-mapped and bump-mapped. The bump mapping adds surface detail to the base, and the lighting and pedestal setup bring out the contrast between the smooth handle and the faceted base.
