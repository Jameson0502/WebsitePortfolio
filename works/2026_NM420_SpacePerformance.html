In the stairwell banging on the railing

In the stairwell banging on the railing, 2026, New Media Performance Art

This sound-based performance transforms an otherwise quiet, overlooked stairwell into an acoustic environment. Using the metal railing as an instrument, I strike the structure with a soft rubber mallet, producing vibrations and sounds that travel throughout the space. The performance explores the relationship between physical action, sound, and architecture, using the natural acoustics of the stairwell to turn an ordinary architectural feature into the primary subject of the performance. I remain silent throughout, allowing the sounds and physical interactions to communicate without verbal explanation.

The performance uses a laptop running Max/MSP, a microphone positioned at the top of the stairwell, and a speaker placed at the bottom. The microphone captures the sounds produced by striking the railing, while Max/MSP provides audio effects and parameters that can be manipulated in real time. By adjusting the processed audio alongside the physical intensity of each strike, I create an evolving sound environment that interacts with the architecture of the space.
