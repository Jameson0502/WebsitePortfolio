Allegra Delivery Droid

Allegra Delivery Droid, 2026, 3D Model

The Allegra Delivery Droid is a 3D model of a to-scale delivery droid, modeled to the exact measurements of real-life delivery droids of this model. The body is wrapped in Allegra-D packaging artwork, with a flat top panel reading "Advertise Your Business Here," a small beacon light on a raised mount, two sensor "eyes" on the front, and chunky wheels on angled supports.

The renders show the droid from several angles against a plain green ground, including close-ups of the front sensors and a low-angle view. I created the model entirely myself, from modeling and UV mapping to lighting. The UV-mapped textures carry the packaging graphics, the ad panel, and the label details across the body, and the lighting brings out the glossy highlights on the trim and wheels.
