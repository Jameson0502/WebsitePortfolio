Red Robot Stop Motion Puppet

Red Robot Puppet, 2025, Stop Motion and Paper

The Red Robot Puppet is a specialized character design and stop-motion animation project developed for a short-form animated piece. The work features a fully articulated, mechanically styled robot character rendered in a striking red and black color scheme with geometric armor plating. Visually distinctive through its clean, modular construction, the character design bridges mechanical precision with tactile stop-motion execution, allowing for fluid articulation within naturalistic environments.

The creative process involved designing, illustrating, and cutting out individual modular components. These components include limbs, torso, head, and an accompanying prop to form a fully pose able puppet template. These physical elements were then integrated into stop-motion scenes, with all photography and image editing handled independently to ensure precise visual continuity and integration with background plates.
