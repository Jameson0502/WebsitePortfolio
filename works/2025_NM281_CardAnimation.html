The Magic Cards

The Magic Cards, 2025, Video Animation

The Magic Cards is a 7-second animated video bumper created in Adobe After Effects that bridges digital motion graphics with a personal interest in Magic: The Gathering. The piece features a detailed tabletop setting complete with a textured wooden surface, a playmat displaying an expansive fantasy landscape, and dynamic cards and game elements that glide and interact across the scene.

The production showcases digital compositing and animation techniques in Adobe After Effects, transforming collectible card game components into a fluid, cinematic motion piece. Through smooth camera movements, precise layering, and atmospheric lighting, the work highlights motion design skills and playful conceptual integration.
