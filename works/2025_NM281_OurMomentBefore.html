Our Moment Before

Our Moment Before, 2025, Video Animation

"Our Moment Before" is an evocative 3-minute experimental video animation that explores the universal human phenomenon of the "moment before". That suspended fraction of a second before a crash, fall, or catastrophic impact where time appears to dilate. Blending multiple genres and a surreal, dream-like atmosphere, the project investigates how contemporary, digital-native consciousness processes mortality and sudden trauma through abstract visual interpretation.

The piece is visually distinctive for its jarring transitions, shifting stylistic genres, and kaleidoscopic warping of everyday perspectives. The creative process integrates captured and found footage such as driving perspectives, explosions, and domestic spaces. Each video rapidly spirals into glitch-like digital effects, chromatic aberrations, and abstract symbolism. By combining digital compositing, video editing, and surrealist pacing, the work demonstrates advanced post-production techniques and a sophisticated conceptual approach to narrative video art.
