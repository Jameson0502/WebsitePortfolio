Spinning Me

SpinningMe, 2025, GIF Animation

SpinningMe is a 23-frame animated GIF featuring a self-portrait that explores movement, repetition, and viewer interaction. Set against a plain white background, the animation captures the artist spinning to the left, using a sequence of still images to create a continuous looping motion. The simple composition places emphasis on the movement and the subject.

The animation incorporates two frames displaying the words Now You! as an invitation for viewers to participate and recreate the spinning motion themselves.
